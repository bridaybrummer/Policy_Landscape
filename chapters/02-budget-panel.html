Budget Panel

Explore the real provincial Health-department budget (EPRE 2024-25), by province, programme, and year.

This chapter explores real provincial Health-department budget data pulled from National Treasury’s Estimates of Provincial Revenue and Expenditure (EPRE), 2024-25 release — 42,578 line items across all 9 provinces, financial years 2020–2026. Some years report multiple budget phases (e.g. FY2023 has Main appropriation, Adjusted appropriation, and Revised estimate all present); the chart and table below use one preferred phase per year (see the code comment in R/load_data.R for the exact priority rule) so totals aren’t double-counted.

Use the filters below to narrow by province, programme, or financial year — both the chart and table update together.

Descriptive statistics & policy framing

A fuller descriptive-statistics pass over this same real data — budget size/growth, provincial distribution, economic-classification and programme composition, the HIV/AIDS+TB subprogramme base rate, and a grounded answer to the anticipated CPHIA “foreign aid vs. local fiscus” question — is browsable as tabbed tables/charts in the Descriptive Statistics chapter (Rscript scripts/run_budget_descriptive_stats.R to reproduce the underlying CSVs). Headline points:

  • ~93% of the Health budget is “Current payments” (staff/goods & services) in both FY2023 and FY2024, leaving under 5% as a capital envelope — any new infrastructure ask (including CRVS-adjacent data-systems asks) competes for a small, largely pre-committed slice.
  • District Health Services is both the single largest programme (~47%) and the one closest to facility-level mortality/notification data — the natural home for a CRVS-data-quality argument aimed at provincial Health budgets.
  • HIV/AIDS + TB subprogrammes account for ~10.6-10.8% of the provincial Health budget (R27.4bn in FY2024) — a real base rate for the excluded population this thesis deliberately doesn’t analyse (see the Plan & Open Questions chapter’s “Future / related projects” section), and it is entirely domestic-government money as far as this dataset can see (EPRE does not capture foreign donor financing at all — see the Descriptive Statistics chapter for the full sovereignty/foreign-aid framing).

Findings are provisional until demonstrated - see the Plan & Open Questions chapter.
